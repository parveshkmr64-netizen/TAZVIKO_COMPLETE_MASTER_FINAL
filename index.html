Frequently asked questions

Common customer and partner questions.

How are payments and merchant settlements handled?
TAZVIKO securely records orders and calculates configured fees/commission. Online payment and automated partner settlements work when the approved payment gateway credentials and payout setup are connected.
Can restaurants and shops add themselves?
Yes. Partners can submit registration, KYC, business details and catalog data. Admin reviews the application before the listing goes live.
Can more languages be added?
Yes. English is the default language. Hindi is available from the language selector, and more languages can be added using the same translation structure.
